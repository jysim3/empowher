Joyce, founder of StellarLuna, specialises in Tarot, Numerology, Astrology and Spiritual Response Therapy (SRT), offering intuitive guidance in a warm and welcoming space for meaningful connection and self-discovery. Complementing these consultations is a curated collection of ethically sourced crystals and handcrafted crystal jewellery. Whether you're seeking guidance through life's transitions or simply looking for a crystal that resonates with you, StellarLuna invites you to pause, reconnect and rediscover your authentic self.

### What inspired you to start your own business, and how has your journey shaped your personal and professional growth?

Having worked in the mental health sector, along with my longstanding interest in holistic wellbeing and the relationship between mind, body and spirit, this has inspired me to create StellarLuna. I wanted to develop a space that brings together intuitive guidance, meaningful conversations and genuine connections with ourselves and others One of my proudest achievements has been turning this interest into an authentic business that offers a range of spiritual services and products that supports my vision, while connecting with people through readings, consultations, markets and community events. The journey has strengthened my confidence, creativity and adaptability, while teaching me the value of patience, consistency and staying true to my own vision.

### How do you balance personal well-being with the demands of running a business, and what strategies work best for you?

I try to practice what I value by making space for pauses, reflection and routines that support my own wellbeing. Planning ahead, setting realistic goals and maintaining clear boundaries help me manage the demands of running a business without feeling that everything has to happen at once. I also make time for exercise where I can, meaningful relationships and activities that help me reset. I have learned that sustainable progress is more important than constantly pushing forward, and that looking after myself allows me to bring greater creativity, energy and presence to StellarLuna.

### What advice would you give to other women seeking to start their own businesses or take their current ventures to the next level?

Start with something that is meaningful to you and allow time for your business to develop. Be clear about what you offer, listen to the people you connect with and do not underestimate the value of showing up consistently. Remember growing a business is not always a linear trajectory. Ask for support, learn from experience and be willing to adapt as you go. More importantly, just make a start and let the rest flow

### What is your go-to word, and what does it mean to you?

My go to Word is "Authenticity" - To me, it represents connecting with yourself, your inner wisdom and people around you in a meaningful, genuine and authentic way..
