Drawing on 15 years of experience in Feng Shui and Qi Men Dun Jia, Jacqueline delivers personalised consultations that integrate traditional Chinese practices with NLP, coaching, and Osho Zen cards. Through thoughtful conversation and reflection, Jacqueline helps clients recognise their strengths, explore new choices, and align their environment to build a deeply fulfilling life.

### What inspired you to start your own business, and how has your journey shaped your personal and professional growth?

My business grew from a desire to support people through changes and uncertainty that I have also experienced. My journey—from engineering to HR and talent development, and later relocating to Australia—has involved finding my direction, adapting to new environments and making decisions without knowing exactly what lies ahead. I understand how valuable it can be to have someone listen and help you see your situation more clearly. Through Yu Liang, I bring together my experience in Feng Shui and Qi Men Dun Jia with NLP, coaching and Reiki to help people understand their energy, reconnect with their strengths and explore a way forward. These experiences have made me more understanding of the different paths people take. They shape how I work: listening without judgement, respecting each person’s pace and offering guidance that connects with their everyday life. I started this business because I want others to feel supported as they navigate their own journey.

### How do you balance personal well-being with the demands of running a business, and what strategies work best for you?

For me, balancing well-being with running a business starts with paying attention to my energy. I’m learning to recognise when I need to pause, rather than keep pushing, and to make space for myself alongside the people I support. Reflection helps me understand what is taking up my energy and what I need. I use Osho Zen cards as prompts to check in with myself, and I’m building a daily practice of gratitude to notice what is going well, even during uncertain times. I also find it helpful to set manageable priorities and give myself permission to adjust my plans. Balance is an ongoing practice—one that allows me to care for myself while continuing to build a business that feels meaningful.

### What advice would you give to other women seeking to start their own businesses or take their current ventures to the next level?

Start by recognising what you already bring—your experience, strengths and the lessons you have learned along the way. What feels ordinary to you may be exactly what someone else needs. Be willing to take a small step before everything feels ready. Speak to people, listen to what they need and allow your business to develop through those conversations. Seek support from people who respect your vision and can offer honest, practical guidance. Most of all, build in a way that honours your energy and values. You can learn from others while finding a pace and direction that suit you. Give yourself room to grow, and remember that your well-being deserves a place in your business plans too.

### What is your go-to word, and what does it mean to you?

My go-to word is “Shine.” When I was studying NLP, I shared a phrase that has stayed with me: “Let’s go into the world and shine.” To me, everyone has a unique light—their strengths, experiences and gifts. Shining means embracing who we are and having the courage to share what we can offer. When we shine, we also bring warmth, encouragement and possibility to the people around us, helping them discover their own light.
