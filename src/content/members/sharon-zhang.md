Sharon Zhang - Founder of Cafe Botanica

Sharon is a vibrant entrepreneur and a beloved community figure. As the founder of Cafe Botanica in Rhodes, she not only manages her bustling café but also dedicates her time to uplifting others. Serving as the Vice President of EmpowHER Sydney Inc., she focuses on empowering women and fostering community enrichment.

### Achievements

Founder of Cafe Botanica: A community hub that welcomes diverse cultures and serves as a gathering place for residents, families, and friends.

Vice President of EmpowHER Sydney Inc.: Actively involved in initiatives aimed at empowering women and supporting community growth.

### Past Work and Career

After more than a decade of experience in the corporate sector, specialising in project management and business development, Sharon decided to pursue her entrepreneurial dreams by establishing her own café. Her background in the corporate environment equipped her with valuable skills in leadership and strategic planning, which she now applies to her café. With a deep passion for gastronomy and horticulture, the idea of a botanical café resonated with her vision.

### Motivation Quote

"Perseverance and hard work pave the way to success. Let's inspire each other to reach for our dreams!"
