**Join Us for a Empowher Women’s Coffee Meetup!**

Are you looking to connect, share stories, and build friendships? Join us for a cozy coffee meetup exclusively for women!

Rhodes EmpowHer Coffee MEETUP

Date: 28th September 2024 Saturday

Time: 9:30am-11:00am

Location: Cafe Botanica. Shop 6, 42 Walker Street Rhodes

This is a great opportunity to relax, enjoy a warm cup of coffee (or tea!), and engage in meaningful conversations with like-minded women. Whether you're new to the area or a long-time resident, everyone is welcome!

**What to Expect:**

Welcoming atmosphere

Inspiring discussions

Networking opportunities

A chance to make new friends

Feel free to bring along your favorite book, a fun story, or simply your lovely self!

Let’s create a supportive community where we can uplift each other over our favorite brews.

Please RSVP to help us reserve enough space.

We can’t wait to see you there!

Our events are kind of free! No entry fees involved. However, we kindly request you to order at least one beverage to support our local cafe.

Photography and videography from the day will be use socials.
