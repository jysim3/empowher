Join us for Australia's EmpowerHER Biggest Morning Tea!

We're excited to be hosting this year's Australia's EmpowerHER Biggest Morning Tea in support of the Cancer Council.

Come together with friends, family, and colleagues for a delicious morning of food, drinks, and community, all while raising funds for an important cause.

Mark your calendars for Saturday 15th June 2024 at 10:30am and stay tuned for more details on how you can get involved.

Together, we can help the Cancer Council continue their life-saving work.

Who's in? Let us know if you'll be joining us for this special event!

Parking: Street Parking Walker Street/Shoreline Drive or Rhodes Central
