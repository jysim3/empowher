Outdoor Boxing Fitness Buddy at Cafe Botanica

Get ready to rumble! Join us at the lush Cafe Botanica for an exhilarating outdoor boxing workout that will have you punching, jabbing, and crossing your way to a fitter you.

This class blends classic boxing techniques with heart-pumping cardio and total-body strength training.

Led by Team Di trainers, you'll learn proper form for jabs, crosses, hooks, and uppercuts as you work up a sweat and tone your muscles.

Grab your workout buddy and get ready to unleash your inner fighter in the fresh air. This fun, beginner-friendly session is the perfect way to challenge yourself, boost your fitness, and make some new friends.

No prior boxing experience is necessary - just bring your enthusiasm, comfortable clothing, towel and a water bottle. We'll provide the gloves, so you can focus on mastering those combos.

This is your chance to punch, kick, and jump your way to a healthier, happier you.

Don't miss out on this unique outdoor workout - sign up today!
