Join us for a relaxing, high-impact craft session at Cafe Botanica! We’re gathering to make handmade crochet heart cards—with 100% of proceeds going directly to EmpowHER funds to support women in need.

Whether you want to learn a new skill, make like-minded friends, or give back to the community, we’d love to have you drop by.

**Event Details**

Date: Thursday 27 August, Thursday 3 September 2026

Time: 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM (Drop in anytime!)

Location: [@](https://www.instagram.com/cafebotanica.au/)[cafebotanica.au](http://cafebotanica.au) Cafe Botanica

Shop 6, 42 Walker Street, Rhodes NSW 2138 Cost: Free (Volunteers needed!)

**What to Expect: **

**Expert Guidance: **Guided by EmpowHER Gold member’s very own Vincenza.

**All Skill Levels Welcome:** Complete beginners are highly encouraged!

**Supplies Provided:** All crochet equipment and materials will be provided on-site. (If you already have your own crochet hooks/needles, feel free to bring them along!)

**Community & Connection:** Enjoy great coffee/ta, meet fantastic people, and create beautiful 3D crochet Heart cards for a meaningful cause.

**Why We're Gathering?**

We're crafting heart cards to raise funds for women in our community. Check out the project we'll be making: Handmade Crochet Heart Card.

Thank you to our supporter [@soulearththerapies](https://www.instagram.com/soulearththerapies/)
