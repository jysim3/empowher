Join Us for Martial Arts (Ving Tsun) for Kids with Autism!

Hey parents! Looking for a fun, engaging activity for your kiddo? Our in-person Ving Tsun classes are designed specially for kids with autism. They’ll learn cool moves, build confidence, and make new friends in a supportive space. It’s all about growth, fun, and empowerment—come see how martial arts can brighten your child’s day!

**Class structure**

All students train together during the warm-up and instruction time.

After warm-up, we use age/ability-based grouping to keep activities comfortable and successful.

**Who this is for**

Kids and teens ages 8–17 with autism spectrum needs (including sensory, communication, and regulation differences).

Suitable for beginners and returning students.

Inclusion & support

The environment is kept as calm as possible.

Students may take a break at any time with staff support.

Parents or Caregivers may observe/assist as needed Safety & Behaviour Notes

No sparring / no full-contact drills.

Gentle partner practice only (opt-in; students can choose to practice solo when needed).

Clear boundaries: respect personal space; communication encouraged.

Sensory considerations: students can bring/plan for comfort items (noise reduction/headphones, fidgets, etc.).
