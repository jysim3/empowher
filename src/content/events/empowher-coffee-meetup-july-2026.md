### Women's Coffee Meetup: A Warm Invitation!

Join us for a delightful gathering where women come together to make new friends, share experiences, and enjoy good company over coffee!

EmpowHer Coffee MEETUP

Date: Thursday 30th July 2026

Time: 9:30am-11:00am

Location: Sip Hour Cafe. 101 The Crescent,

Homebush West NSW 2140, Australia

3 mins walk from Flemington Train Station)

**What to Expect:**

- **Connect & Catch Up:** Meet new faces and reconnect with familiar friends.
- **Share Your Passions:** Bring your favourite recipes to share, and showcase your cooking skills!
- **Book Discussions:** Dive into inspiring reads and exchange recommendations.

Whether you're a seasoned professional, a budding entrepreneur, or simply looking to expand your circle, you are **warmly welcomed!**

**Why Attend?**

- **Inspiration & Support:** Gain new insights and encouragement from empowered women like you.
- **Personal & Professional Growth:** Discover opportunities to learn and grow together.

When women come together, **anything is possible!**

We can’t wait to see you there!

Our events are kind of free! No entry fees involved. However, we kindly request that you to order at least one beverage to support our local cafe.
