Join us for a FREE rejuvenating session of "Yoga for Women's Self-Care," designed specifically to nurture and empower women. This event offers a unique blend of yoga postures, breathwork, and meditation techniques tailored to meet the needs of women at all stages of life.

**Event Highlights:**

Gentle Yoga Flow: Experience a series of gentle and restorative yoga postures that help release tension, improve flexibility, and enhance overall well-being.

Breathwork Techniques: Learn powerful breathing exercises that promote relaxation, reduce stress, and balance your energy levels.

Meditation Practices: Engage in guided meditations that foster inner peace, clarity, and emotional resilience.

Self-Care Tips: Receive practical self-care tips and techniques that you can easily incorporate into your daily routine.

Community Connection: Connect with like-minded women in a supportive and nurturing environment.

**Who Should Attend:**

Women of all ages and fitness levels seeking to enhance their physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

Those new to yoga as well as experienced practitioners looking to deepen their self-care practices.

Anyone interested in learning holistic techniques to manage stress and promote overall health.

Benefits:

- Improved physical health and flexibility

- Reduced stress and anxiety

- Enhanced mental clarity and focus

- Increased emotional balance and resilience

- Greater sense of community and support

**Details:**

**Yoga Teacher: Runjhun from The Living Art**

Date & Time: Sun 25 Aug, 10 am - 11 am

Location: Cafe Botanica, Shop 6/42 Walker St, Rhodes

What to Bring: Comfortable clothing, yoga mat, water bottle, and an open heart

Join us for a transformative experience that will leave you feeling refreshed, empowered, and more connected to yourself. Don't miss this opportunity to invest in your well-being and embrace the power of self-care through yoga.
