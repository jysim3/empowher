The EmpowHER Network is organising a coffee morning at Café Botanica, Rhodes.

It's a great opportunity for women, business owners, and entrepreneurs in the area and beyond to meet and mingle. You can share ideas, make new connections, and enjoy a cuppa in a relaxed setting. Whether you're a professional or just starting, this event is perfect for networking, building relationships, and making new friends. We're here to support each other in empowering you.
We would love for you to join us at this event on Saturday 31st August 2024
Please RSVP to let us know if you'll attend.
Rhodes EmpowHer Coffee MEETUP
When: 31st August 2024 Saturday
Time: 9:30am-11:00am
Location: Cafe Botanica.
Shop 6, 42 Walker Street Rhodes
Street parking available: Walker Street and surrounding area
Host by: Chiral Yadava (Life Coach)
Hope to see you there.
Our events are kind of free! No entry fees involved.
However, we kindly request you to order at least one beverage to support our local cafe.
