- **AGM for Paid Official Members Only: Time:** 5:30 PM **Voting:** For official paid members only
- **Anniversary Celebration:Time:** 6:30 PM-8:30 PM <strong>Open to All Members, Friends, and Family</strong> Enjoy an evening filled with fun, fellowship, and festivities!

**Tickets:**

- **Official Paid Members:** $25/per person
- **Non-Members:** $45/per person
Ticket covers 1 welcome drink + refreshment
Lucky door prizes

This is a wonderful opportunity to connect, reflect on our achievements, and look forward to another year of making a difference together. We can't wait to celebrate with you!
