Always wanted to learn to play mahjong? Join us at Mahjong for Beginners. Whether you're curious to learn a new game or simply looking to meet new people, come along on 6 August, 7.30–9.00 pm, for a relaxed session of mahjong, friendly conversation, and the opportunity to make new connections in our community.

No experience is needed—everyone is welcome.

Every Thursdays

**Date:** Thursday 13, 20, 27 August 2026

**Time:** 7:30 pm – 9:00 pm

**Location:** Cafe Botanica, Shop 6/42 Walker St, Rhodes NSW 2138

**What to Expect:**

No Chinese language knowledge or mahjong experience required

Step-by-step guidance in a friendly, supportive environment

Meet new people and enjoy a relaxed, social evening Whether you're coming on your own or with a friend, everyone is welcome.

**Why Attend?**

Expand your social circle and connect with other women in the community

Learn a fun and engaging game that keeps your mind active

Enjoy a relaxing evening away from your usual routine

Discover a hobby you can continue to enjoy with others

We'd love to see you there!

strong>Cost:</strong> Our meetup is free to attend. We simply ask that you purchase at least one drink to support our local café.
