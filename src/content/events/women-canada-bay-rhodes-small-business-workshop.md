Free Small Business Workshop for Women.

In our Women CANada Bay Small Business Workshop Part I, you can expect an inspiring and motivating experience as speakers share their leadership journeys. They will provide valuable insights and tips on leveraging platforms like LinkedIn and GoDaddy to enhance your brand and grow your small business.

One important aspect covered in the workshop is business bookkeeping. You will learn best practices for effectively managing your financial records, which is vital for the success of any business.

Overall, the workshop aims to equip you with practical knowledge and inspiration to drive the growth and success of your small business.

Attention, Women CANada Bay & Rhodes EmpowHer Members!

We are excited to announce an upcoming event that you won't want to miss! Mark your calendars for Wednesday, May 29, 2024, from 6-8pm, as Women CANada Bay presents the "Small Business Workshop."

Join us for an evening filled with valuable insights and inspiration!

What to expect?

Expert speakers

Personal leadership journey

Expert guidance and insights

Empowerment and confidence

Tips on LinkedIn and GoDaddy

SME ATO record keeping

Interactive Q&A

Open Mic

Our special guest for the event is none other than Tara Commerford, the former CEO of Go Daddy. Tara will be sharing her remarkable journey and offering expert tips on navigating the world of small businesses and startups.

But that's not all!

We are also thrilled to have Susan Ngan from Tax Store joining us for a book keeping & tax return Q&A session. Susan's expertise in the field will provide an excellent opportunity for you to ask burning questions and gain further insights.

Don't forget to save the date and invite your friends and colleagues to this incredible event. Let's empower each other and unlock the potential of our entrepreneurial dreams!
