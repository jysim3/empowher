**When:** Thursday 12th March 2026

**Time:** 11:30am-2:00pm

**Where:** Amora Hotel

Level 1, 11 Jamison St, Sydney NSW 2000, Australia

**Price:** $99 (Limited to 50 guests)

**Guest Speaker**

**Neuroscience & Nervous System Regulation**

Dr. Leanne Elich will share:

- How chronic stress rewires the brain

- how to reverse it

- Practical tools to regulate your nervous system in real time

**Guided Breathwork: A 3-Minute Reset**

Simon Yuen will lead a guided breathwork session designed to:

- Calm the nervous system quickly

- Shift out of fight-or-flight

**Environment, Energy & Focus**

Join Jane Langof in a live Q&A with MC Bronwyn Williams, exploring:

- How your environment influences mood, energy, and productivity

- Simple Feng Shui shifts for home and office spaces

- How space can either drain you - or actively support you

Your environment is not decoration.

It’s a regulation tool.

**Experiential Wellness Activations**

Throughout the event, guests will have access to:

- Onsite wellness massage (short, intentional resets)

- Antioxidant scanning (insight into how your body is responding to stress)

- A mini wellness gift pack to support ongoing regulation

**Meet Neurielle**

Each guest will receive Neurielle, a playful wellness companion gifted by Dr. Leanne.

Neurielle symbolises:

- Curiosity over pressure

- Regulation over hardening

- Energy over exhaustion

- A simple reminder to: Pause. Regulate. Create more good days.
