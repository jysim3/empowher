Free Speech

Members have the right to express their opinions and share their experiences freely within the group.

While the group values open dialogue, all members are encouraged to communicate respectfully and thoughtfully.

Group Responsibility

Empowher Sydney Inc is not responsible for the opinions expressed by individual members.

Statements made by members do not reflect the views or positions of the group as a whole.

Complaints or grievances regarding individual members' statements should be addressed directly with the member in question and not the group.
