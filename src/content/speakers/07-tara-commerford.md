Tara Commerford has held leadership roles in some of the world’s largest media and technology companies including LinkedIn and GoDaddy. She started her career as editor of the local newspaper in Five Dock. Tara was listed in the 2023 Women of the Year Awards and has been a finalist in the CEO of the Year awards. Come along and be inspired by her story of leading through industry changes and shifts. Walk away with tips on how to expand your network and grow your small business online.
