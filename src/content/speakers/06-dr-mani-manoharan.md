Dr Mani Manoharan is Consultant Endocrinologist working in Liverpool and Fairfield Hospital. She also works in Castle Hill and Wentworthville Private rooms. Her speciality is Women's Health in Endocrinology. She is passionate about improving health literacy for women in prevention health.( Diabetes and Obesity prevention) Perimenopause and Menopause is vulnerable period in Women's life span. Her focus is to assist women morally and health wise when going through this phase of life.
