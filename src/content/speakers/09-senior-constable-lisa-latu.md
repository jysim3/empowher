Lisa Latu is a crime prevention officer with the New South Wales (NSW) Police Force. In this role, she works with communities to implement strategies aimed at reducing crime and enhancing public safety. Her responsibilities include conducting risk assessments, delivering crime prevention education, and coordinating community initiatives. In addition to her professional role, Lisa is the primary parent to her two children, Elaina, and Junior. She balances her duties in law enforcement with her responsibilities as a mother, demonstrating commitment and professionalism in both areas of her life.
