Saskia Vromans is the Place Manager Rhodes for the City of Canada Bay Council. Over 20 years, Saskia’s professional career has spanned the industries of health, non-profit arts and local government sectors. Her passion is in working alongside communities to transform spaces into places; developing creative solutions to supporting community building and tackling the challenges of living in contemporary urban environments.
