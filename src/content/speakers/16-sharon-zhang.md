Sharon is the founder Cafe Botanica Rhodes.Having amassed over a decade of experience in the corporate sector, Sharon made the strategic decision to pursue her entrepreneurial aspirations by establishing her own café. With a profound passion for gastronomy and a penchant for horticulture, the concept of a botanical café resonated deeply with her. While acknowledging the numerous challenges inherent in this endeavor, she is steadfast in her conviction that diligent perseverance will ultimately lead to success, step by step. Come and meet her in person to hear more about journey.
