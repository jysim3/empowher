Minki the founder of Merci Boutique and a resident of Rhodes for 5 year.

She fulfils the roles of a mother, a wife, and a businesswoman. Minki is a very passionate and caring woman. With 2 stores in Rhodes and Eastwood and a team of 8 employees, she has found success in her business ventures. What sets Minki apart is her dedication to helping and supporting disabled ladies and single moms, as she is deeply passionate about making a positive impact in their lives. She is an amazing lady.
