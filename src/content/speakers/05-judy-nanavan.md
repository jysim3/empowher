Judy is a Nutritionist and Metabolic Balance Coach with a special interest in menopause and perimenopause. She is passionate about health and wellbeing and believes that good nutrition is the cornerstone of health.

Judy is non-judgemental and very understanding and compassionate towards you and your health concerns. She takes a scientific and holistic approach to your health which encompasses using the healing power of nutrition, sustainable eating strategies, exercise and other lifestyle modifications, delivering sustainable treatments for optimal and ongoing wellness. She believes in educating you and empowering you to have control over your own health and wellness.

Judy earned her Bachelor of Health Science in Nutrition and Dietetic Medicine in 2017 after following her own health journey to find holistic health. She says it is the perfect blend of her love for good food, science and leading a healthy lifestyle.
