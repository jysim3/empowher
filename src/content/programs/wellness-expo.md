### Good to know

### Frequently asked questions

###### Do I need to register in advance?

Entry is free — tap [Register Now](https://www.empowhersydney.com/event-details/empowering-wellness-expo-a-fun-day-for-the-whole-family/form).

###### Is the venue accessible?

The Connection Event Space is wheelchair accessible, with accessible restrooms on-site. There are two accessible street parking spots directly outside The Connection on Shoreline Drive. Street access to the venue is flat and step-free.

###### How do I get there without driving?

Rhodes Station is a few minutes' walk on Walker Street, and buses 410, 458, 533 and 526 all service the area. Walking or cycling in via the Homebush Bay Circuit and Bennelong Bridge is another easy option.

###### Is there parking on-site?

Timed street parking (2–4 hours) runs along Shoreline Drive right by the venue. Rhodes Central Shopping Centre on Marquet Street or Rhodes Waterside Shopping Centre on Rider Boulevard also offer three hours free parking.

###### Do I have to be there for Lucky Door Prize Draw?

Yes. Please refer to our program for our lucky door prize draw.

###### What is the purpose of this raffle?

All proceeds from ticket sales directly support EmpowHER Sydney Inc. and its ongoing community programs, which focus on supporting women, families, and local wellness initiatives.

###### Where can I buy raffle tickets during the pre-sale?

Pre-sale tickets are available at Café Botanica Rhodes from 20–25 September 2026 4pm. Additional tickets will also be sold at the Empowering Wellness Expo on Saturday, 26 September 2026 (subject to availability).

*Cafe Botanica opening hours Sun 9am-5pm, Wed-Fri 10am-9:30pm.

###### How much do raffle tickets cost?

Tickets are priced at $5 for 1 ticket or $10 for 3 tickets.

###### Who can buy raffle tickets?

The raffle is open to NSW residents aged 18 years or older.

###### When and where will the raffle draw take place?

The draw will take place on Saturday, 26 September 2026 3:30pm, during the Empowering Wellness Expo at The Connection, 30 Shoreline Drive, Rhodes.

###### Do I need to be present at the raffle draw to win?

No. Attendance at the Empowering Wellness Expo is not required to win.

###### How will I know if I’ve won?

Winners will be announced at the event where practicable and will be contacted directly via the SMS or email provided on their ticket stub within 3 business days.

###### What do I need to claim my prize?

Winners may be required to present a valid photo ID and their matching winning ticket stub to claim their prize.

###### Can I exchange my prize for cash?

No. Prizes are non-transferable, non-exchangeable, and cannot be redeemed for cash, unless required by law.

###### What happens if a prize is unclaimed?

If a prize remains unclaimed after reasonable contact attempts, it will be handled in accordance with applicable NSW regulatory standards.

###### How is my personal information used?

Personal details collected on ticket stubs are used solely to administer the raffle, notify winners, and meet legal record-keeping obligations. Your information will not be used for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.
