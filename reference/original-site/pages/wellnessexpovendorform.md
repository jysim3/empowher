### Community Event

#### A Fun, Uplifting Day for the Whole Family!

Looking for the perfect weekend plan to enjoy with your loved ones? Join us for the Empowering Wellness Expo, presented by EmpowHER Sydney Inc. This vibrant community event is designed to bring generations together for a day packed with inspiration, self-care, and fun. Whether you want to learn practical health tips, discover local small businesses, or enjoy a joyful day out with your partner, kids, or grandparents—there is a space here for everyone.

#### What to Expect on the Day:

🛍️ The Wellness Market: Browse and shop from fantastic local vendors showcasing mindful crafts, holistic skincare, and self-care essentials.

🏃‍♂️ Fitness Corner: Get moving! Participate in fun mini-workouts, movement demonstrations, and discover practical ways to keep your body active and strong at any stage of life.

🎨 Kids' Corner: Bring the little ones! A dedicated, fun space packed with engaging activities, games, and entertainment to keep the children smiling while you explore.

👩 Women’s Health Network: A supportive, empowering space connecting women with trusted resources, expert insights, and community support for physical and mental well-being.

👨 Men’s Health Network: A dedicated hub focusing on the health priorities that matter most to men, offering practical advice on fitness, lifestyle, and mental resilience.

👵 Older People Information Desk: A friendly, helpful resource hub designed to guide seniors and families towards active ageing, community care services, and holistic wellness support.
