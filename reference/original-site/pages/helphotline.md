## HELP HOTLINE

For confidential information, counselling and support, we recommend below.

This is a free and confidential service that isn’t part of EmpowHER Sydney Inc. If you need an interpreter or translator, you can ask for one and the counsellor will make the arrangements.

If you are concerned for your safety or the safety of others, seek immediate assistance by calling Triple Zero (000).
