### Highlights from Community Mental Health Day

For a few hours, a room full of strangers became a community — sharing honest stories, sitting in stillness together, cheering each other on, and reminding one another that it's okay to not be okay.

None of it would have happened without our volunteers, speakers and the EmpowHER crew working quietly behind the scenes, and the sponsors and supporters who believed in this day.

To everyone who walked through the door — thank you for showing up for yourself, and for each other.
