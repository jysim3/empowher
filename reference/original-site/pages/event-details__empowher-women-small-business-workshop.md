### About the event

Welcome to the EmpowHER Women Small Business Workshop!

Join us for an exciting event at Cafe Botanica where you can connect with like-minded women entrepreneurs, learn valuable business strategies, and be inspired to take your small business to the next level. There will be speakers, open mic and networking opportunities focused on topics like digital marketing, website development, sourcing, branding, and marketing tips.

Our workshop will feature:

Insightful sessions on key topics for women entrepreneurs

Interactive discussions to share experiences and best practices

Valuable networking opportunities to build meaningful connections
