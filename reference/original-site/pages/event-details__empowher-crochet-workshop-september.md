### About the event

Join us for a relaxing, high-impact craft session at Cafe Botanica! We’re gathering to make handmade crochet heart cards—with 100% of proceeds going directly to EmpowHER funds to support women in need.

Whether you want to learn a new skill, make like-minded friends, or give back to the community, we’d love to have you drop by.
