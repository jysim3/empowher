Are you a wellness entrepreneur, healthcare professional, or small business owner looking to showcase your brand to an engaged, passionate local community?

EmpowHER Sydney Inc. is absolutely thrilled to announce that applications are officially open for stallholders and vendors at our upcoming Empowering Wellness Expo! For the very first time, we are bringing this vibrant family health and wellness day to the beautiful community of Rhodes.

Our mission is to bring generations together to celebrate health, happiness, and well-being. We are looking to partner with local businesses and passionate experts who share our vision of helping families thrive.

#### Why Exhibit at the Rhodes Wellness Expo?

👋 Direct Local Exposure: Connect face-to-face with hundreds of local families, residents, and wellness enthusiasts right in the heart of Rhodes.

🤝 Specialised Health Hubs: Align your business with our dedicated zones, including the Women’s Health Network, Men’s Health Network, Older People Information Desk, Fitness Corner, and Kids' Corner.

📈 Grow Your Brand: Build your customer base, generate new leads, network with other local business owners, and sell your product or services directly on the day.

#### What Type of Vendors Are We Looking For?

We are curating a diverse, high-quality lineup of stalls to provide incredible value to our attendees. We would love to hear from businesses specialising in:

🌿 Holistic health, skincare, and natural wellness products

🏃‍♂️ Fitness, movement, and physical health coaching

🧠 Mental wellbeing, mindfulness, and self-care resources

🍎 Healthy nutrition, snacks, and dietary supplements

🎨 Mindful crafts, handmade goods, and eco-friendly products

👵 Community care services and resources for active ageing

#### How to Apply:

Stall spaces are limited to ensure a balanced and successful variety of vendors. If you are ready to elevate your business and support community wellness, we would love to have you on board!

Applications are quick and easy to complete online. Simply click the link below to tell us about your business and secure your spot.

👉 [Click Here to Apply as a Vendor Now!](https://www.empowhersydney.com/wellnessexpovendorform)

Have questions about stall spaces or sponsorship opportunities? Feel free to reach out to the EmpowHER Sydney team. Let’s work together to inspire and empower our community!
