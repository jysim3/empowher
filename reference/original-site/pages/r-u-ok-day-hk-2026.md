### WHAT IS R U OK? DAY

### A conversation could change a life

### R U OK? started in Australia as a national day to remind us that any day is a good day to check in on the people we care about.

### It is built on a very simple idea: asking "Are you OK?" and truly listening can help someone who is going through a tough time. You don't need to be an expert to reach out, just showing that you care can make a huge difference.

### Now, we are bringing this movement from Sydney to Hong Kong for the very first time. Together, let's start the conversation and support one another.

### CONNECT

### ANNIEWHO

### Founder, EmpowHER Sydney

### Executive Producer and Founder, Anniewho Productions

Annie founded EmpowHER Sydney to give women a genuinely safe space — somewhere to talk openly about mental health, lift each other up, and share real stories without judgment.

Her drive comes from a deep care for people who suffer in silence, particularly around domestic violence and the stigma that keeps people from asking for help.

Outside EmpowHER, Annie's career bridges East and West: raised and educated in Sydney with roots in Hong Kong, she has spent over 20 years building brands and producing media across Australia, Europe, China, Hong Kong, Japan, Ireland and Switzerland as Founder of Anniewho Productions Ltd — work recognised by Bloomberg Asia, the Wall Street Journal, SCMP, and a 40 Under 40 award.

### ENGAGE

### MATTHEW

### OWEN

### Illusionist

### Sleight of Hand Artist

Matthew Owen is a British sleight of hand artist and magical entertainer who has been based in Hong Kong since 2011.

Known for close-up, stage and corporate magic, he has held resident magician roles at venues including Lips Cocktail & Theatre at Grand Hyatt Hong Kong and Tosca at The Ritz-Carlton, and has performed for politicians, dignitaries and celebrities across Hong Kong, India and the Philippines.

Matthew's warm, family-friendly style makes him a natural fit for bringing lightness and play to a day about mental wellbeing.

### THRIVE

### TERESSA

### sIU

### International Wellness Speaker

### Educator and Former TV Journalist

Teressa Siu is an international wellness speaker, mental health educator and award-winning former broadcast journalist, having served as Special Health Correspondent for CGTN (CCTV) after a career covering health and medical news across Asia.

She is a certified yoga teacher and holistic health coach, and created "Nutrition-Based Wisdom," her own curriculum for staying grounded through holistic, sustainable living.

Her work in health journalism and advocacy has been recognised with honours including a Women's Leadership Award for Excellence in Broadcast Journalism and a place among 50 Outstanding Women in Healthcare.

### LEARN

### SONIA

### SAMTANI

### Clinical Hypnotherapist

### NLP Trainer

### TEDx Speaker

Sonia Samtani is the Founder and CEO of the All About You Centre, Hong Kong's first English-language hypnotherapy and mental wellness facility.

A licensed clinical hypnotherapist, NLP trainer, qualified life coach and mental health counsellor, she has worked with more than 40,000 people worldwide over two decades and was formerly a senior mentor to success coach Tony Robbins.

Sonia is a two-time TEDx speaker and best-selling author of 52 Thoughts for Conscious Living, and her talk on beating burnout draws on her work helping people move from anxiety and overwhelm toward genuine, lasting balance.
