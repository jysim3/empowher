Great news, EmpowHER community! We are absolutely thrilled to announce that we will have an official stand at the upcoming The 7 Wonder Shifts Book Launch & Wellness Expo on Saturday, 25th July 2026!

If you’ve been looking for the perfect opportunity to connect with us in person, chat about your wellness journey, and experience the supportive energy of our community firsthand, this is your invitation.

#### What is the Event?

Hosted by Wonderlab, this incredible one-day, family-friendly wellness festival is celebrating the official book launch of The 7 Wonder Shifts. It’s a beautifully curated space designed to help you slow down, embrace curiosity, and explore holistic well-being. Think of it as a massive celebration of mindfulness, growth, and community connection.

#### Event Details:

When: Saturday, 25th July 2026

Time: 9:00 am – 4:00 pm

Where: Writing NSW, Callan Park (Lilyfield, NSW)

#### 🛍️ Shop with Purpose: The EmpowHER Merchandise Store

We are also incredibly excited to share that we will be showcasing our EmpowHER Merchandise Store right at our stand! You’ll be able to browse and purchase our exclusive collection in person.

Best of all? 20% of all profits from our merchandise go directly to the EmpowHER funds. Natural Essential Spray, Anti-microbial socks, Japanese Matcha and Gift of Love. Every single purchase you make directly supports our mission, helping us provide vital resources, programs, and aid to women in need across our community. It’s the perfect way to treat yourself or a loved one while making a real, tangible impact.

#### Why You Should Stop By the EmpowHER Stand:

We’re bringing the heart of EmpowHER right to the expo floor! When you swing by our stand, you can:

Meet members of the EmpowHER team and connect with like-minded women.

Learn more about our upcoming Sydney programs, community events, and resources.

Shop our purposeful merchandise range to support women in need.

Grab some exclusive insights (and maybe some special treats!) we’ve prepared just for the day.

Whether you're a long-time member of the EmpowHER sisterhood or completely new to our community, we would love to see your smiling face. It’s going to be a beautiful day filled with inspiring conversations, wellness discovery, and positive vibes.

#### Grab Your Tickets!

The festival is just around the corner, so make sure to lock in your spot. Head over to the official event page to check out the full schedule and secure your tickets:
