## Join Us as a Volunteer

At EmpowHER Sydney, our vibrant community of volunteers is at the heart of everything we do. From event logistics and community outreach to specialised mentoring and digital marketing, every volunteer plays a vital role in helping us empower, support, and elevate women across Sydney.

Whether you're looking to give back, connect with inspiring peers, or build new skills in a supportive environment, we would love to have you on board.

Please complete the application form below to let us know more about your background, interests, and availability. A member of our team will be in touch when an opportunity matching your profile opens up!
