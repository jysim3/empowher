## Get Involved

Welcome to EmpowHER Sydney — a safe, inclusive space dedicated to creating real connection, collaboration, and personal and professional growth for all women. We believe in the power of every woman's unique journey, and our mission is to build an environment where every voice matters and every woman can thrive.

Whether you are looking to share your story, lend your skills, partner with us, or join our supportive network, there are many ways to make an impact.

Select how you would like to get involved below to access the relevant form.

Join as a Volunteer

Lend your time, talents, and enthusiasm to help run our community programs, events, and initiatives.

[Join us](https://www.empowhersydney.com/volunteer)

Become a Member

Join our vibrant network to access networking events, business support, and peer mentorship.

[Join us](https://www.empowhersydney.com/pricing-plans/list)

Apply as a Keynote Speaker

Share your professional insights, personal journey, or well-being strategies at our upcoming events.

[Apply now](https://www.empowhersydney.com/keynotespeakerform)

Become a Sponsor or Partner

Collaborate with us to champion women's empowerment while aligning your organisation with meaningful community impact.

[Support us](https://www.empowhersydney.com/sponsors)
