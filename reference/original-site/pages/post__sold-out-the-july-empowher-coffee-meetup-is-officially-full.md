Wow, what an incredible morning! We are still buzzing with gratitude following our completely sold-out June Coffee Meetup.

At EmpowHER Sydney, our heart beats for connection, and seeing our community gather, share stories, and lift each other up over a warm cuppa is exactly why we do what we do. The energy in the room was absolutely electric, filled with laughter, inspiring conversations, and the beautiful sound of women making genuine, lasting connections.

Whether you were a familiar face or stepped out of your comfort zone to join us for the very first time, thank you for bringing your warmth and open heart to the table. You are what makes this tribe so incredibly special!

#### Highlights From the Morning:

🎲 The EmpowHER Bingo Game: We kicked off the morning with a lively, customised EmpowHER bingo game that had everyone moving, chatting, and discovering surprising things they had in common with one another.

⏱️ Speed Friending Fun: Our fast-paced, high-energy speed friending icebreaker was an absolute hit! It completely broke the ice, took the pressure out of mingling, and ensured everyone got to chat with plenty of new faces.

☕ Meaningful Conversations: From celebrating small wins to offering support through life’s hurdles, the room quickly transformed into a safe, uplifting space for raw and real discussions.

✨ Community Growth: With every ticket snapped up, our community continues to prove that when women support women, magical things happen.

#### Missed Out This Month? Don't Worry!

Because our meetups are intimate spaces designed for real conversation, tickets do sell out incredibly fast. If you couldn't secure a spot for June, we would love to welcome you to our next gathering!

Our community is always growing, and there is always a seat at the table waiting for you. Keep an eye on our events page and social media so you can grab your ticket early for the next one.
