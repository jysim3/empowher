Nutritionist and Metabolic Balance Coach

Judy Nanavan

Judy is a Nutritionist and Metabolic Balance Coach with a special interest in menopause and perimenopause. She is passionate about health and wellbeing and believes that good nutrition is the cornerstone of health.

Judy is non-judgemental and very understanding and compassionate towards you and your health concerns. She takes a scientific and holistic approach to your health which encompasses using the healing power of nutrition, sustainable eating strategies, exercise and other lifestyle modifications, delivering sustainable treatments for optimal and ongoing wellness. She believes in educating you and empowering you to have control over your own health and wellness.

Judy earned her Bachelor of Health Science in Nutrition and Dietetic Medicine in 2017 after following her own health journey to find holistic health. She says it is the perfect blend of her love for good food, science and leading a healthy lifestyle.

National Manager – Client Relationship Services & Senior Consultant Psychologist

Emma McMillan

Emma with 12 Years experience is a Clinical Psychologist with extensive experience in Clinical, Organisational, and Forensic settings. She specializes in Psychological Assessment, Diagnosis, and Treatment, focusing on Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, and Crisis Management. Emma works with adults facing mood disorders, anxiety, trauma, and gender identity issues. She has successfully provided services to various stakeholders, including military personnel. With leadership experience, she develops effective training programs that enhance workplace psychological health and resilience. Emma’s engaging approach makes psychological concepts accessible, ensuring lasting impact in diverse organisational contexts.

Multicultural Community Liaison Officer

Nelly Sinha

Nelly Sinha is a dedicated and passionate professional serving as a Multicultural Community Liaison Officer with NSW PF at Burwood Police Area Command. With a diverse background and a deep understanding of various cultures plays a pivotal role in fostering strong bonds between law enforcement and the linguistically and diverse communities within NSW.

After working in the corporate sector for many years, Nelly joined NSWPF over 10 years ago. Her passion and dedication saw the development of the Dowry Abuse Program (DAP) along with two work colleagues. The project has strived to improve understanding and communication between police and Indian subcontinent communities residing in NSW in the area of dowry abuse.

Most recently DAP won the award for Excellence in Law Enforcement Initiative for Women in the Community Award at the Australasian Council of Women & Policing (ACWAP) 2022 Excellence in Policing Awards on 19 October 2022. They were nominated and shortlisted for the NSPW Police Officer of the Year Awards in April 2022.

Beyond work, Nelly remains actively engaged in community affairs, participating in various multicultural events and running art and craft classes.
